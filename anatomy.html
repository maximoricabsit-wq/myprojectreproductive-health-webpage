Male Reproductive Anatomy

  • Testes — produce sperm and testosterone.
  • Scrotum — sac that holds the testes and keeps them at optimal temperature.
  • Epididymis — stores and matures sperm.
  • Vas deferens — transports sperm to the urethra.
  • Seminal vesicles & prostate — add fluid to sperm to form semen.
  • Urethra & Penis — pathway for semen and urine; organ for intercourse.

Female Reproductive Anatomy

  • Ovaries — produce eggs (ova) and hormones (estrogen, progesterone).
  • Fallopian tubes — site of fertilization and egg transport.
  • Uterus — where an embryo implants and pregnancy develops.
  • Cervix & Vagina — birth canal and passageway.
  • Vulva — external female genitalia.
